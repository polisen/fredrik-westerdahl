# Institutional measure

> White field, black type, almost nothing else. The page should feel like an institution, not a personality.

## The idea

Restraint is the design. No cards, no pills, no chrome, no color. Atmosphere may exist only if it is nearly invisible. Motion is allowed when it is the playhead — one gesture, one number — not decoration.

## How it shows up

- White background, black text. That is the palette.
- The ASCII field stays, but blue and pink collapse toward white. A monochrome breath, not a scene.
- Type is the layout. Spacing does the work that cards used to do.
- A face is permitted. A header is not.
- The footer is text: PDF, GitHub, email.
- Funny is out. Measured is in.
